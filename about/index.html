How I approach technical editing and programming.

When I edit, I look at both the sentence and the job it needs to do. A reader following a procedure needs to know what to do, what to expect, and where to look when something goes wrong.

Programming gives me another way to work through those questions. A repeatable setup or a small script can make a recurring task easier to run and easier to explain.
